The Inner Line Permit (ILP) is the first thing you need to sort out before visiting Mizoram — and the good news is that it takes about ten minutes online, costs less than ₹400 total, and will arrive in your inbox within 48 hours. This guide covers everything: what the ILP actually is, which documents to prepare, how to apply online step-by-step, where it gets checked, how to extend it inside the state, and what foreign nationals need to do instead. By the time you finish reading, the permit will be the least of your planning concerns.

What is the Mizoram Inner Line Permit?

The Inner Line Permit is an official travel document issued by the Government of Mizoram under the Bengal Eastern Frontier Regulation of 1873 — a colonial-era law that India retained after independence to protect the cultural identity, land rights, and ecological integrity of its northeastern tribal states. Without a valid ILP, Indian citizens from outside Mizoram cannot legally enter or stay in the state, check into any hotel, or pass through road checkpoints.

Mizoram is one of four states in Northeast India — along with Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, and Manipur — that currently enforces the ILP system. Think of it less as a bureaucratic hurdle and more as a measure that has helped Mizoram remain one of the safest, least-commercialised, and most culturally intact destinations in the country. The permit directly protects the communities you will be visiting.

Quick fact: As a Mizoram-based tour operator, Mizo Travel handles ILP assistance for all guests on our packages. You share your ID details, we do the paperwork — usually completed 7 to 10 days before your arrival. That said, solo travellers can absolutely apply themselves; this guide shows you exactly how.

Who needs a Mizoram ILP?

The rule is straightforward: every Indian national who is not a permanent resident of Mizoram must carry a valid ILP at all times while in the state.

No exceptions for infants or children: All individuals travelling to Mizoram, including minors, must be covered by a valid ILP. Parents can include a child's details on their own application form, but the permit must list every person in the group.

Types of Mizoram ILP and fees

Two types of ILP are issued. For tourists visiting Mizoram, the Temporary ILP is what you want. The Regular ILP is for longer-term stays such as extended business, work, or research.

Temporary ILP 15 days Renewable once, for another 15 days
Regular ILP 6 months Renewable twice (6 months each)
Application fee (Temporary) ₹120 Paid online at application
Application fee (Regular) ₹220 Paid online at application
Entry fee (at checkpoint) ≈ ₹350 Paid on arrival; carry cash
Renewal fee ₹20 At DC office inside Mizoram

Validity starts from entry date, not issue date. Your permit is valid for 15 days from the day you cross into Mizoram at the checkpoint, not from the date shown on the permit approval. If your plans shift, you do not need to reapply — just adjust your travel date within the stated travel window on the application.

Documents needed for the Mizoram ILP

The documentation requirements are refreshingly simple. You need one of the following government-issued photo ID documents — any one is sufficient:

  • Aadhaar card — Most commonly accepted; fastest to upload as it's already a digital document
  • Voter ID card — Accepted, but the image quality on older physical cards can cause upload rejections
  • Indian passport — Accepted; useful if Aadhaar is unavailable
  • Driving licence — Accepted; ensure both front and back are scanned in a single file
  • PAN card — Accepted as a secondary ID in some cases; Aadhaar is preferred

In addition to your ID, you will need:

  • A passport-size photograph — Digital format (JPG, PNG under 200KB); clear background, recent
  • Your travel dates — Planned entry and exit dates for Mizoram
  • Accommodation address — Hotel name or homestay name and location in Mizoram (a booking confirmation helps, though it is not mandatory)
  • Purpose of visit — Select "Tourism" from the dropdown in the online form

Carry physical copies, not just the digital permit. Once approved, print at least two copies of the permit. Checkpoints along the highway and at hotel check-in counters require a physical copy. Network connectivity in many parts of Mizoram — particularly east of Aizawl toward Champhai — is unreliable, so a phone screen may not be accessible when you need it.

How to apply for the Mizoram ILP online (step-by-step)

The official Mizoram ILP portal is ilp.mizoram.gov.in. The entire application takes about 10 minutes. Here is the exact process:

1

Open the official ILP portal

Navigate to ilp.mizoram.gov.in on any browser. This is the only official Mizoram government portal for ILP applications. Ignore third-party sites charging service fees — the permit itself has a nominal government fee and does not require an agent.

2

Register a new account

Click "New Applicant" or "Register." Enter your email address and mobile number. You will receive an OTP on both — use it to verify and create your login credentials. Keep these saved, as you will log back in to check application status and download the approved permit.

3

Fill in the application form

After logging in, select "Apply for ILP." Choose Temporary ILP for tourism. Fill in: your full name as it appears on your ID, date of birth, permanent address, and the names and ID details of everyone travelling with you (spouse, children, companions). All co-travellers must be listed on the same application.

4

Enter your travel details

Specify your planned date of entry, date of exit, purpose (Tourism), your port of entry (Vairengte road border, Lengpui Airport, or Sairang Railway Station), and your accommodation address inside Mizoram. If you are on a multi-destination tour, enter the first night's hotel name in Aizawl.

5

Upload documents and photograph

Upload a scanned copy or clear phone photo of your government ID and your passport-size photograph. The portal accepts JPG and PDF formats. Keep each file under 200KB — if uploads fail, compress the image using any free online tool and retry. Both documents should be clearly legible.

6

Pay the processing fee

Pay ₹120 (Temporary ILP) or ₹220 (Regular ILP) through the integrated payment gateway. UPI, debit cards, credit cards, and net banking are all accepted. Save the payment receipt — you may need it if there is any discrepancy at the checkpoint.

7

Submit and note the reference number

Review all details carefully before submitting. After submission, you will see a confirmation screen with an application reference number. Note this number — you can use it to track your application status on the portal under "Check Status."

8

Download and print the approved permit

Once approved (typically within 24–48 hours on working days), you will receive an email notification. Log back into the portal, go to "My Applications," and download the approved permit as a PDF. Print at least two copies. Keep one in your travel wallet and one in your bag — separate from each other.

Apply at least 7 days before travel. While most applications are processed within 48 hours, applying a week in advance gives you buffer time to resolve any document rejection or technical issues without changing your travel dates. During public holidays and festival seasons, processing can take up to 72 hours.

Offline alternatives: Mizoram Houses and border counters

If you cannot apply online, three offline routes exist — though none is as reliable as the online process.

Mizoram Liaison Offices (Mizoram Houses)

Mizoram Liaison Offices — often called Mizoram Houses — in major Indian cities issue ILPs in person. Visit the nearest office with your ID, two passport-size photographs, and your travel details. Processing is usually same-day. Offices are located in:

  • Kolkata — Mizoram House, 3 Russel Street (nearest major hub for eastern India travellers)
  • New Delhi — Mizoram House, Chanakyapuri
  • Guwahati — Mizoram House (convenient for travellers arriving via Assam by road)
  • Silchar — Mizoram House (closest to the road border at Vairengte)
  • Shillong — Mizoram House (useful for Meghalaya connections)
  • Mumbai — Mizoram House, Bandra

On-arrival counters (limited, not recommended)

ILP counters operate at Lengpui Airport (Aizawl) during flight arrival times, at Sairang Railway Station, and at the road border crossing at Vairengte on the Assam-Mizoram highway. These counters can process your permit, but they come with real risks: limited operating hours, long queues during peak season, frequent power or connectivity outages, and no fallback if the counter closes before you reach it. If you rely on on-arrival processing and the counter is closed, you will not be allowed entry into the city. Apply online in advance — it takes ten minutes and eliminates all these variables.

Where your Mizoram ILP will be checked

Unlike some permits that are a one-time formality at entry, the Mizoram ILP is actively checked at multiple points throughout your visit. Here is where to expect it:

✈️ Air entry Lengpui Airport Checked immediately after landing, before baggage claim exit
🚂 Rail entry Sairang Station Checked at the station gate on arrival
🛣️ Road border Vairengte Checkpoint Main Assam–Mizoram road border; all vehicles stopped and permits verified
🏨 Every hotel All accommodation Legally required to photocopy your ILP at check-in — no permit, no room
🚔 Roadblocks Highway checkpoints Particularly active on routes toward Champhai and the Myanmar border
🏛️ Tourist sites Entry points & parks Some national parks and border-adjacent sites may request your ILP

Being caught without a valid ILP is a punishable offence. You can be fined, detained, or turned back at any checkpoint. Hotels will refuse to check you in. The enforcement is active and consistent — not a formality. This is not the permit to leave to chance.

Extending your Mizoram ILP inside the state

If your trip runs longer than planned, a Temporary ILP (15 days) can be extended once for an additional 15 days — giving you up to 30 days total. Extensions are processed at the Deputy Commissioner's (DC) office in the district where you are currently staying.

What you need for an extension:

  • Your original ILP — The printed permit issued when you entered
  • One passport-size photograph — Same specifications as the original application
  • Photocopy of your government ID — Aadhaar or the same ID used for the original ILP
  • Extension fee — ₹20 (paid at the DC office cashier counter)

Extensions are almost always processed the same day, often within a couple of hours. Visit the DC office as early in the morning as possible. The DC offices in Aizawl and Champhai are the most visited by tourists; our guides can accompany you if needed. Do not let your permit expire before applying for the extension — there is no grace period.

Foreign nationals: the Protected Area Permit (PAP)

Non-Indian passport holders cannot apply for an ILP. The permit for foreign nationals visiting Mizoram is the Protected Area Permit (PAP), governed directly by India's Ministry of Home Affairs.

Important update (December 2024 onwards): India reimposed the PAP requirement for foreign nationals in Mizoram (along with Nagaland and Manipur) in December 2024, reversing a relaxation that had been in place since 2011. Foreign nationals can no longer register on arrival — the PAP must now be obtained in advance. Plan for a processing lead time of at least 2 to 4 weeks before your entry date.

How foreign nationals apply for the Mizoram PAP

  • Via the e-FRRO portal: Apply at indianfrro.gov.in/frro if you are already in India. Submit at least 15 days before your intended entry into Mizoram, as processing can take up to two weeks.
  • Via a registered Indian travel operator: This is the most reliable route. A registered operator applies to the Ministry of Home Affairs on your behalf and handles all documentation. Mizo Travel assists all international guests with PAP applications — contact us at least 3 weeks before your travel date.
  • Via an Indian Mission abroad: Apply at the Indian Embassy or Consulate in your home country before travelling to India. This adds significant lead time but is an option for those not yet in the country.

Common ILP mistakes — and how to avoid them

After running tours in Mizoram for years, our team has seen every permit problem in the book. These are the ones that actually strand people:

  • Applying the day before travel. Online processing typically takes 24–48 hours on working days. Apply at least 7 days before you travel. If you apply on a Friday and the government office has a holiday on Monday, you may not receive approval until Tuesday — which could delay your entire trip.
  • Wrong entry dates on the application. Your ILP is valid only for the travel window you specify. If you enter the state one day before the listed start date, your permit is invalid. Double-check your arrival date and build in one extra day as a buffer if your transit connections are tight.
  • Not listing all co-travellers. Every person in your group — including children — must be listed on the ILP. If you add a travel companion after submitting, you need a new application covering them. One permit does not cover unnamed travellers.
  • Not printing copies before leaving Aizawl. Internet connectivity drops significantly once you leave the city, particularly on the route to Champhai and Zokhawthar. Print copies in Aizawl — before departing for any eastern or southern circuit.
  • Letting the permit expire mid-trip without extending. There is no grace period. If your 15-day permit expires while you are still in Mizoram, visit the nearest DC office immediately. Do not wait until a checkpoint catches it.
  • Using unofficial third-party sites. Several websites charge ₹500–₹2,000 in "service fees" to submit a form that takes 10 minutes on the official government portal for ₹120. The official portal is ilp.mizoram.gov.in — use it directly.
