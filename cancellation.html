How to cancel your booking

Three steps. The cancellation date is the day we confirm receipt of your written request — not the day you intended to send it.

01

Write to us

Send a cancellation request by WhatsApp to +91 95316 71758 or by email to hello@mizotravel.com. Include your booking reference, travel date, and full name.

02

We confirm receipt

We will acknowledge your cancellation in writing within 24 hours. Your cancellation is not effective until we send this confirmation. If you don't hear back within 24 hours, follow up — messages can occasionally go astray.

03

Refund is processed

Any eligible refund is returned via the same payment method used at booking (UPI, bank transfer, or Wise) within 7 business days of the written confirmation. You will receive a cancellation summary with the refund amount.

Verbal cancellations are not accepted. A cancellation is only valid when submitted in writing (WhatsApp message or email) and acknowledged by Mizo Travel in writing. Phone calls are useful for queries but do not constitute a formal cancellation.

Rescheduling your trip

If your dates change but you still want to visit Mizoram, a reschedule is almost always the better option.

One complimentary reschedule

Every confirmed booking includes one free date change, provided the request is made more than 14 days before your original travel date and the new dates are available. No fee, no penalty — just a new confirmation.

Second reschedule or short-notice changes

A second reschedule, or any reschedule requested within 14 days of your travel date, is treated as a cancellation of the original booking and a fresh booking. Standard cancellation fees apply to the original booking based on the notice given. The new booking requires a new deposit.

Validity of rescheduled bookings

Rescheduled travel must take place within 12 months of the original booking date. Rescheduled bookings are subject to any price differences in accommodation, guide, or transport costs applicable to the new dates. We will notify you of any price adjustment before confirming the new dates.

Best time to reschedule: Contact us as soon as you know your dates may change — even a day's extra notice can make a meaningful difference to the fee tier that applies. Our team is reachable on WhatsApp within a few hours on any working day.

If Mizo Travel cancels your tour

We take confirmed bookings seriously. Operator-initiated cancellations are rare, and when they happen, we stand fully behind our commitment to you.

If Mizo Travel cancels a confirmed booking for reasons within our control — guide unavailability, vehicle failure, operational issues on our side — we will contact you as early as possible and offer your choice of:

  • Full refund of all amounts paid, including the deposit, returned within 7 business days.
  • Rescheduled trip at no additional cost, subject to your availability and our capacity.
  • Alternative package of equivalent or superior value, if we can arrange it within a similar timeframe.

We will not cancel a confirmed booking to take a higher-paying guest or for commercial convenience. If we ever have to cancel, it will be for a genuine operational reason and you will be the first to know.

Notification: We will inform you of any operator-initiated cancellation by WhatsApp and email as soon as the decision is made — always with a minimum of 48 hours' notice where circumstances allow.

Force majeure & unforeseeable events

Mizoram's geography means some disruptions are genuinely outside anyone's control. Here's how we handle them.

Force majeure refers to events beyond the reasonable control of either party that make it impossible or unsafe to operate a tour as confirmed. Examples include:

Floods & landslides that close road access to trekking routes or destinations

Severe weather rendering trekking routes unsafe for the duration of the trip

Government travel restrictions — curfews, permit suspensions, or border closures

Civil unrest or political events that affect public safety in the state

Public health emergencies or epidemics that trigger government movement restrictions

Natural disasters including earthquakes or wildfires affecting access routes

What happens in a force majeure situation

  • We will contact you as soon as we become aware of the event affecting your tour.
  • We will make every reasonable effort to reschedule your trip to the earliest available dates.
  • Where a reschedule is not possible, we will refund the portion of your payment not yet committed to third-party suppliers (guides, homestays, transport). This amount varies case by case and a full refund cannot always be guaranteed.
  • The deposit remains non-refundable in force majeure situations, as it covers planning and administrative costs already incurred.

This is why we strongly recommend travel insurance. A comprehensive policy covering trip cancellation, curtailment, medical evacuation, and natural disasters will protect you in situations where our policy cannot offer a full refund. Mizo Travel does not sell insurance — we suggest comparing policies on platforms like PolicyBazaar or purchasing through your bank before travel.

Partial & mid-trip cancellations

Sometimes guests need to cut short a trip that's already begun. Here's what applies.

Early departure by guest choice

If you choose to leave a tour early for personal reasons unrelated to safety, no refund is applicable for unused days, accommodation, or services. This is because our partners — particularly village homestays — have turned away other guests to hold your booking.

Early departure due to safety or medical emergency

If you must leave a tour early due to a genuine medical emergency or an unsafe situation, Mizo Travel will assist you in arranging transport back to Aizawl and, where possible, onward connections. In these circumstances, we will review unused accommodation and service costs on a case-by-case basis and issue a partial refund where recovery of those costs from our partners is possible.

Guide-initiated route changes

If a guide modifies or abandons a route for safety reasons during a trip (e.g. a trail becomes impassable due to landslide or flash flood), we will arrange alternative activities or accommodation of equivalent standard where possible. No refund applies for changes made in the interest of guest safety.

Travel insurance is especially important here. Most comprehensive travel insurance policies cover trip curtailment — meaning you can claim the cost of unused days from your insurer rather than absorbing the loss yourself.

Travel insurance

Not mandatory. But it's the single thing that would have helped every guest who ever came to us with a difficult situation.

We strongly recommend comprehensive travel insurance for every Mizoram trip.

Mizoram is remote. The nearest hospital to Phawngpui is several hours by road. Monsoon landslides close routes without warning. A policy that costs a few hundred rupees per day can protect thousands. Look for cover that includes all of the following:

  • Trip cancellation and curtailment (covers unused days if you must leave early)
  • Emergency medical expenses and hospitalisation
  • Emergency evacuation and repatriation
  • Personal baggage and equipment loss
  • Adventure activities cover (essential for trekking packages)
