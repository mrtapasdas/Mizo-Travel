Published itineraries are planned around normal conditions. Mizo Travel reserves the right to make changes to any itinerary before or during a trip where necessary for safety, logistical, or operational reasons. We will always communicate changes to you as early as possible.

Minor changes

Significant changes

Our guides have the authority to modify or abort any activity or route in the interest of your safety. Their decision on the ground is final. No refund is applicable for activities altered or cancelled due to unsafe conditions.

Permits & Documentation

Entry to Mizoram requires travel permits for all visitors. Mizo Travel assists with permit applications as a standard part of every booking — this assistance is included in the package price.

Inner Line Permit (ILP) — Indian nationals

All Indian nationals (other than permanent residents of Mizoram) require a valid Inner Line Permit to enter the state. We process ILP applications on your behalf. You must provide accurate personal details (full name as on government ID, ID number, travel dates) within the timeframe we specify — typically at least 10 days before travel. Mizo Travel is not liable for permit refusals or delays caused by inaccurate information provided by the guest or by government processing times.

Protected Area Permit (PAP) — Foreign nationals

Foreign nationals require a Protected Area Permit to visit Mizoram. We coordinate PAP applications in conjunction with ILP processing. Additional processing time of up to 21 days may be required for foreign nationals. We recommend foreign guests initiate the booking process at least 30 days before their intended travel date.

Guest responsibilities

  • Carry your ILP or PAP (physical or approved digital copy) throughout your stay. Government checkpoints can ask to see permits at any time.
  • Carry original government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, passport, or voter ID) at all times.
  • Comply with all permit conditions, including restrictions on photography near border zones and restricted areas.
  • Inform Mizo Travel immediately of any changes to your travel dates after a permit has been issued, as permits are date-specific.

Travelling without a valid permit is a serious offence under the Inner Line Permit system and can result in detention, fines, and deportation. Mizo Travel cannot be held responsible if a guest travels to areas outside their permitted zone or with an expired permit.

08

Guest Conduct

Mizo Travel keeps groups small and works closely with village communities. The conduct of guests directly affects those communities and our ability to continue operating in them. We expect all guests to:

  • Respect local customs, traditions, and religious practices. Mizo society is predominantly Christian; please dress modestly in villages and at churches.
  • Follow all instructions given by your guide, particularly regarding safety, restricted areas, and cultural sensitivity.
  • Treat host families, village residents, and all local service providers with courtesy and respect.
  • Avoid littering on trails, in forests, or in villages. Carry-in carry-out applies on all trekking routes.
  • Refrain from consuming alcohol in villages where it is not customary or where your guide advises against it.
  • Not enter restricted areas or cross into Myanmar without the correct documentation and prior approval from Mizo Travel and the relevant authorities.
  • Not photograph individuals without their explicit consent, particularly in villages and cultural settings.

Removal from tour: Mizo Travel and its guides reserve the right to remove any guest from a tour at any time for behaviour that is unsafe, disrespectful to local communities, or in breach of these terms. In such cases, no refund will be provided and the guest will be responsible for their own onward transport and accommodation costs.

Health & Fitness

Mizoram's terrain is hilly and demanding, even on non-trekking itineraries. Guests are responsible for assessing their own fitness and health before booking a tour.

Medical disclosure

You must disclose any medical conditions, disabilities, allergies, dietary requirements, or medications that may affect your ability to participate in the activities included in your package. This information is treated confidentially and used only to ensure appropriate support is arranged. Failure to disclose relevant medical information releases Mizo Travel from responsibility for any consequences arising during the trip.

Fitness requirements

Our trekking packages — particularly the Phawngpui Blue Mountain route — involve multi-day hiking at altitude, with steep ascents and variable trail conditions. Guests must be in good cardiovascular health and capable of hiking for 6–8 hours per day with a daypack. If you are unsure whether a route is appropriate for your fitness level, speak to us before booking.

Medical facilities

Mizoram's remote areas have limited medical facilities. The nearest hospital to routes like Phawngpui may be several hours away. Mizo Travel guides carry basic first aid kits and are trained in wilderness first response, but they are not qualified medical professionals. Guests with serious medical conditions should ensure they have adequate travel insurance including medical evacuation cover.

Medications: Carry all prescription medications in sufficient quantity for your entire trip, plus a buffer for delays. Specialised medications may not be available in Aizawl or more remote areas.
